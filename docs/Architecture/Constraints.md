# Technical Constraints

AMIRIS shall be:

* platform-independent and should run on the major operating systems (Windows, Linux, and Mac-OS),
* runnable from the command line,
* developed under a liberal open-source license - licences of third party libraries have to be compatible with the licenses of AMIRIS

# Organisational Constraints

Since AMIRIS is developed by the German Aerospace Center (DLR), there shall always be a fork under its control.
DLR personnel shall be owner of this fork and shall have the right to dismiss merge requests not suiting its cause.
All contributors to AMIRIS must sign a contributors license agreement (CLA), granting DLR rights to their contributions.
Each AMIRIS component repository shall include this [Contributors License Agreement](./CLA) file.
